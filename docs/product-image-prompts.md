# Tankroy Product Image Prompts

These prompts are for generating one consistent catalog image for each fictional Tankroy product. Every product and specification is synthetic.

## Shared Art Direction

Use the following visual rules for every image:

- Photorealistic premium industrial product photography
- Square 1:1 composition, ideally 1600 × 1600 pixels
- Three-quarter front view, product centered, fully visible, generous margin
- Soft light-gray seamless studio background with a subtle floor shadow
- Tankroy visual language: matte graphite enclosure, deep teal accent, one restrained amber status light
- Realistic molded polymer, powder-coated metal, fasteners, seals, and connectors
- Clean product suitable for a modern B2B ecommerce catalog
- No people, tanks, pipes, vehicles, scenery, packaging, diagrams, or floating components
- No existing brand, certification mark, readable label, typography, watermark, or generated text
- Leave a small clean blank badge area so the product name can be rendered by the website rather than generated inside the image
- One product only unless the prompt explicitly describes a kit

Save the selected results using the filenames shown below and convert the final catalog assets to WebP.

## Selection Checklist

Before accepting an image, verify:

- The product is fully visible and centered.
- The background and camera angle match the rest of the catalog.
- No accidental company name, real logo, certification, watermark, or readable generated text appears.
- Connectors and mechanical details look physically coherent.
- The product remains visibly fictional and does not copy a recognizable commercial device.
- The image is safe to crop to a square card and a wider product-detail layout.
