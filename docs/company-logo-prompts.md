# Fictional Company Logo Prompts

These prompts create simple, reusable logo marks for the fictional companies in TankFit AI. They are deliberately designed as symbol-only SVG or transparent PNG marks: the company name should be rendered as accessible HTML text by the application, not baked into an AI-generated image where lettering may be distorted.

## Shared Art Direction

Use these rules for every logo:

- Clean flat vector logo mark, not a product photograph
- Transparent background
- 1:1 canvas, ideally 1024 × 1024 pixels
- Simple geometry that remains recognizable at 32 × 32 pixels
- Maximum of three solid colors, no complex gradients
- Strong contrast in both light and dark UI themes
- No mockup, business card, sign, website screenshot, or 3D presentation
- No readable words, letters, initials, numbers, trademark symbols, flags, or certification marks
- No resemblance to an existing company, sports team, government seal, or known trademark
- Deliver an SVG when the generator supports clean vector output; otherwise use a transparent PNG

After generation, export the final assets using the exact filenames below. The website will render the company name beside each mark.

## 1. Tankroy Systems Inc.

**Filename:** `tankroy-systems.svg`

> Design a clean fictional technology-company logo mark for Tankroy Systems Inc. Combine a simplified rounded storage tank silhouette with three ascending wireless signal arcs, using deep navy, deep teal, and one small amber accent. The tank should be abstract and geometric, with a stable base and a single centered circular level indicator. Make the mark feel reliable, precise, and industrial without looking like a real sensor brand. Flat vector geometry, transparent background, strong silhouette at small sizes. No text, letters, initials, numbers, logos, watermark, gradients, or certification symbols.

## 2. AirFlame Fuels

**Filename:** `airflame-fuels.svg`

The mark should feel dependable, regional, and operational, with balanced geometry and a clear silhouette at 32 pixels. Flat vector geometry, transparent background. No text, letters, initials, numbers, fuel-pump imagery, real logos, watermark, gradients, or certification symbols.

## Selection Checklist

Before accepting each logo:

- It is an original fictional mark and does not resemble a recognizable real brand.
- It works on both light and dark backgrounds.
- It remains legible as a favicon-sized mark.
- It contains no generated words or malformed lettering.
- It has a transparent background and clean edges.
- It can be displayed beside the company name rendered in the interface.
