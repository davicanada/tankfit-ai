# Asset Provenance

## Scope

The TankFit AI catalog contains 13 fictional product images and four fictional company marks. Every depicted company, product, specification, visual identity, and commercial context is synthetic and was created exclusively for this independent personal project for the Jornada de Dados competition.

No asset represents, reproduces, or was derived from a real employer's catalog, product, customer, internal system, or confidential information.

## Generation and Review

- **Generation model:** Grok 4.6, as identified by the project owner in the image-generation workflow.
- **Creative direction and selection:** Davi Almeida.
- **Product prompts:** [`product-image-prompts.md`](product-image-prompts.md).
- **Company-mark prompts:** [`company-logo-prompts.md`](company-logo-prompts.md).
- **Post-generation review:** The selected assets were checked for visual consistency, fictional branding, readable generated text, watermarks, obvious real-world trademarks, file format, dimensions, and safe repository use.

The product images were exported as 1600 x 1600 WebP files. The company marks are symbol-only SVG files with transparent backgrounds and no embedded scripts, remote references, raster images, or text elements.

## Usage Notes

Product names and company names are rendered as accessible HTML by the application rather than being embedded in the generated artwork. The assets are portfolio and demonstration materials and do not constitute product photography, technical documentation, certification evidence, or representations of commercially available hardware.
